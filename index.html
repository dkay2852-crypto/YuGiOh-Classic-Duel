How to Play

Free Duel: Pick Classic or Duelist Kingdom, then Free Duel, then Yugi, Kaiba, Joey, or Custom Deck. A duelist opponent is one of the other two. A custom deck faces Yugi, Kaiba, or Joey at random.

Tournament: Pick Classic or Duelist Kingdom when the run starts. Continue keeps that choice, and the island shows it. You start with 2 Star Chips. Weevil, Rex, Mai, Keith, and Panik, then a rival (Kaiba, or Yugi if you are playing Kaiba), hold 1, 1, 1, 2, 1, and 2 Star Chips. Beating each once reaches exactly 10, which opens Pegasus's castle. The island always shows how many chips you still need. A loss costs 1. At 0 the run is over. If you have beaten everyone on the path and still have fewer than 10, rematch those duelists: a win pays their chips again, and a loss still costs 1. Each first win opens a booster from that duelist's set. Progress is saved on this device. Deck on the island swaps the deck for the next duel without erasing the run. Each arena keeps its field in both rule sets. In Duelist Kingdom that field gives +30% ATK and DEF. Opponents get sharper along the path. Pegasus reads your hand with the Millennium Eye. Beating him unlocks Exodia and the Egyptian Gods, and the main menu can switch to Battle City.

Card Binder: The main menu opens every card in the classic pool, grouped by set, in a grid. Owned cards show their art. Extra copies show a count (x2, x3). Missing cards are a dark silhouette. Each set, and the whole binder, shows a completion percent. Search and Filters narrow the grid by name, type, attribute, monster type, level, ATK, DEF, rarity, foil, and owned or new. Sort by name, ATK, DEF, level, or newest. Tap a card to see each version you own. From the deck builder, that same card can be added or removed.

Rematch: After you beat a duelist, fight them again from their island stop or from Rematch on the main menu. The deck is meaner and the plays are sharper. A win pays more Duel Points than the first time and the pack is more likely to hold a rare. Star Chips stay put, except when the path is clear and you still need chips for Pegasus. Those island rematches pay the same chips as the first win, and a loss costs 1.

Ante: Settings has an Ante rule, off unless you turn it on. Before a tournament duel, you and the opponent each put up one card. You put up a spare card from your collection. The last copy a saved deck needs stays, and preset Yugi, Kaiba, and Joey decks are never edited. The winner takes the loser's card. Both cards show before the duel, and the result shows after.

Custom decks: The builder lists cards you own in a grid, and it will not add more copies than you have (max 3). A count shows when you own more than one. Search by name, then open Filters for Monster, Spell, Trap, attribute, monster type, level, ATK and DEF ranges, rarity and foil, owned or new, and sort. Owned, Main, and Extra switch which pile you are looking at. Auto-Build fills a legal 40-card deck from the strongest cards you own. A new collection starts with a 40-card pile of commons, so one legal deck is possible right away. Preset Yugi, Kaiba, and Joey decks stay playable and do not add their cards to the collection. Binder in the builder opens the full pool. A main deck is 40–60 cards. Fusion Monsters go in the Extra Deck (max 15). Save up to 8 decks on this device. Rename, copy, or delete them, and pick which one to use before a free duel, a tournament, a rematch, or the next duel in a run. An older saved deck still loads. The Forbidden/Limited switch is off unless you turn it on.

Goal: Drop their Life Points to 0, or deck them out.

Phases: Draw → Main 1 → Battle → Main 2 → End. Next Phase advances. You do not draw on your first turn.

Classic: 8000 LP. Level 5–6 needs 1 tribute; 7+ needs 2. Egyptian Gods need 3. No attacks on the very first turn. Polymerization can use monsters from your hand or field.

Duelist Kingdom: 2000 LP, no tributes. The field gives matching Types +30% ATK and DEF. The first player may attack. Polymerization uses monsters on the field only.

Exodia: After you beat Pegasus, the five pieces can go in a deck. Holding all five in your hand wins, in either rule set.

Fusion: Activate Polymerization to summon from your Extra Deck. Tap Extra to look at it. Yugi fuses Gaia the Dragon Champion, Kaiba fuses Blue-Eyes Ultimate Dragon (and Rabid Horseman), Joey fuses Flame Swordsman.

Time Wizard: Call Heads or Tails. Right: the opponent’s monsters are destroyed, and Baby Dragon becomes Thousand Dragon. Wrong: your monsters are destroyed and you take damage equal to half their ATK.

Equips: Legendary Sword (Warrior), Book of Secret Arts (Spellcaster), Dragon Treasure (Dragon), Black Pendant, Horn of the Unicorn. Tap Equip, then a face-up monster.

Kuriboh can be discarded so an opponent’s attack deals 0. Swords of Revealing Light stops their attacks for 3 of their turns. Shield & Sword swaps ATK and DEF until the end of the turn. Panther Warrior must tribute a monster to attack.

Story: A short scene plays when you arrive, before and after each tournament duel, and after you beat Pegasus. Tap to advance, or Skip. Settings can skip every scene. Win and loss have different lines.

New cards: The first copy of a card, from a booster, Grandpa's shop, an ante, or another reward, shows a NEW badge and a glow when it is revealed. The binder keeps NEW on that card until you tap it. A green count on Card Binder is how many of those you have not opened yet. Cards you already owned are treated as seen.

Daily Puzzle: One puzzle for today's date. The board, hands, and Life Points are already set. Win that turn with the rules named on the puzzle, Classic or Duelist Kingdom. Hint and Retry sit on the duel screen. The first solve of the day pays 40 Duel Points. Solving on back-to-back days raises a streak. Reset Progress clears the streak.

Card Shop: Grandpa sells booster packs for Duel Points, from the main menu or the shop stop on the island. A daily deal, picked from today's date, sells one card at a discount. A foil deal shows SR, UR, or SCR on the card, with the same colored border as the rest of the game. Tap the card picture to read it. Sell lists each spare separately: a normal copy, then Super, Ultra, and Secret. It will not sell a copy a saved deck still needs, and it asks before the card leaves. Wins pay more against harder duelists. Staying at half your Life Points or higher pays a bonus. A loss pays a little. A set unlocks when you beat its duelist. Duel Points are saved on this device. Reset Progress clears them.

During a duel: Tap Log for every action. Hold a card to read its full text, ATK, DEF, and type. Each opponent brings their own arena behind the cards. Settings saves your rules, animation speed, sound, story skip, cinematics, ante rule, and main-menu theme on this device.

Foils: Booster packs and Grandpa's daily deal can pull a Super Rare, Ultra Rare, or Secret Rare foil. Secret is the rarest. A foil card shows SR in silver, UR in gold, or SCR on a rainbow, with a matching border, plus the shine. Commons have no badge. The binder legend uses the same marks. Tap a card to see how many normal, SR, UR, and SCR copies you own. A foil copy and a normal copy both count as that card in the deck builder. The same badge shows on the pack, in the binder, in the builder, in your hand, on the field, and in the shop. The first time an older save opens, each extra copy you already owned — not the first copy, and not cards that only live in a preset deck — gets one roll at the normal pack foil odds. Counts stay the same. That check runs once.

Intros: A VS screen slams in before each duel, with both names, ace art, and the opponent's line. It moves on after about two seconds, or when you tap. Blue-Eyes White Dragon, Dark Magician, Red-Eyes Black Dragon, and a completed Exodia play a short flash. Turn Cinematics off in Settings to skip them.
