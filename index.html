How to Play

Goal: Reduce the opponent’s Life Points to 0, or deck them out.

Phases: Draw → Main 1 → Battle → Main 2 → End. Use Next Phase to advance.

Classic: One Normal Summon/Set per turn. Level 5–6 needs 1 tribute; 7+ needs 2. Change position once per turn (not the turn summoned).

Duelist Kingdom: 2000 LP, no tributes, and a random field boosts matching Types by +30% ATK/DEF.

Spells/Traps: Dark Hole, Raigeki, Monster Reborn, Pot of Greed, Trap Hole, Mirror Force, Fissure, Change of Heart.

Tap a card for a close-up and actions. Tap a monster in Battle phase to declare an attack.
