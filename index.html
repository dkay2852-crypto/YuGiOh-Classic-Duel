How to Play

Start: Pick Classic or Duelist Kingdom, then Start Duel, then Yugi, Kaiba, or Joey. The opponent plays one of the other two decks.

Goal: Drop their Life Points to 0, or deck them out.

Phases: Draw → Main 1 → Battle → Main 2 → End. Next Phase advances. You do not draw on your first turn.

Classic: 8000 LP. Level 5–6 needs 1 tribute; 7+ needs 2. Polymerization can use monsters from your hand or field.

Duelist Kingdom: 2000 LP, no tributes. A random field gives matching Types +30% ATK and DEF. Polymerization uses monsters on the field only.

Fusion: Activate Polymerization to summon from your Extra Deck. Tap Extra to look at it. Yugi fuses Gaia the Dragon Champion, Kaiba fuses Blue-Eyes Ultimate Dragon (and Rabid Horseman), Joey fuses Flame Swordsman.

Time Wizard: Call Heads or Tails. Right: the opponent’s monsters are destroyed, and Baby Dragon becomes Thousand Dragon. Wrong: your monsters are destroyed and you take damage equal to half their ATK.

Equips: Legendary Sword (Warrior), Book of Secret Arts (Spellcaster), Dragon Treasure (Dragon), Black Pendant, Horn of the Unicorn. Tap Equip, then a face-up monster.

Kuriboh can be discarded so an opponent’s attack deals 0. Swords of Revealing Light stops their attacks for 3 of their turns. Shield & Sword swaps ATK and DEF until the end of the turn. Panther Warrior must tribute a monster to attack.
